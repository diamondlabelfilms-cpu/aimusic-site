# AI Music Pipeline

AI Music Pipeline is a private publishing tool operated by Diamond Label Films. It prepares and publishes our own original music videos and clips to our own social media channels (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok).

It is not a consumer product and does not offer accounts, logins, or services to the public. Only our own creator accounts connect to it.
